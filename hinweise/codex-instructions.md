# Mila French DST Practice - Codex Instructions

## Goal

Create French practice worksheets for Mila, Grade 4, bilingual French-German
school, German track.

The worksheets should follow the style of French primary-school evaluations,
not beginner conversational French.

Use Quarto-compatible Markdown.

## General Formatting Rules

- Use clear section headings.
- Use numbered exercises.
- Leave enough blank space for handwritten answers.
- Use simple French instructions.
- Use age-appropriate vocabulary.
- Prefer short sentences.
- Use themes such as:
  - magic
  - dragons
  - pirates
  - animals
  - school
  - holidays
  - adventures

## Exercise Types

### 1. Verbs in Context

Do not only ask for isolated conjugation tables.

Prefer sentence-based exercises.

### 2. Identify the Conjugated Verb

Students should practice finding the conjugated verb in a sentence.

### 3. Find the Infinitive

Students should identify the infinitive form of a conjugated verb.

### 4. Subject / Verb / Complement

Students should identify sujet, verbe, and complément.

### 5. Homophones

Include common French homophones. Focus on:

- `on` / `ont`
- `son` / `sont`
- `et` / `est`
- `a` / `à`

### 6. Tense Recognition

Students should recognize past, present, and future.

### 7. Agreement in the Noun Group

Students should practice gender and number agreement.

### 8. Reading Comprehension

Use short texts with simple but complete questions.

Students must answer in complete sentences.

### 9. Written Production

Include one short writing task in the future tense.

## Recommended Worksheet Structure

A complete worksheet should usually include:

1. Conjugation in context
2. Verbs in sentences
3. Infinitives
4. Sujet / Verbe / Complément
5. Homophones
6. Past / present / future recognition
7. Gender and number agreement
8. Reading comprehension
9. Short writing task

## Important Pedagogical Note

Avoid worksheets that only test isolated vocabulary or beginner phrases.

The school style is closer to French primary-school grammar practice.

The goal is to train Mila to:

- recognize grammar inside sentences,
- manipulate verbs,
- identify sentence structure,
- write complete answers,
- justify with `parce que` or `car`,
- produce a short coherent text in the future tense.
